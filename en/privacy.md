This site sets **no cookies** and serves no advertising. Here is, service by service,
what goes through third parties when you read it.

## Audience measurement: GoatCounter

To know which posts are read and where readers come from, the site counts its visits
with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), an independent, open-source service
**with no cookies and no persistent identifier**. It keeps only totals: the page viewed,
the site you came from, the browser, the operating system, the screen size and the
country. Your IP address and browser are used for the day's count but are not stored;
no person can be identified from this data. Visits are not matched with any other site.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages) (GitHub, United
States), which may log visitors' IP addresses for the security of the service.

## Email subscription

The "Subscribe" form passes your address through [Formspree](https://formspree.io/).
The author keeps it outside any marketing service and uses it only to announce a new
post, in blind copy. To unsubscribe, simply reply to one of those messages.

## Libraries and external content

To display formulas, charts and icons, pages load libraries from
[jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/) and Font Awesome; the share buttons come from
[AddToAny](https://www.addtoany.com/). The videos on the [Videos]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/reels.html)
page are embedded from YouTube in "no cookie" mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`). Like any
web server, these servers receive your IP address when the content loads.

## The author's tools for his YouTube channels

The "Audience" application is the author's personal tool. It runs on his computer and is
offered to no one else. With his authorization, it uses the YouTube API Services for two
things, and only on **his own two channels**
([@energy_alternatives](https://www.youtube.com/@energy_alternatives) in French and
[@energy_alternatives_en](https://www.youtube.com/@energy_alternatives_en) in English):

- **reading the channel's statistics** (views, subscribers, watch time), to display them
  on his computer;
- **publishing his own videos**: uploading a video, its thumbnail and its captions,
  adding it to one of the channel's playlists and posting a first comment.

It accesses no other user's data and collects no personal data; what it reads is not
published, shared or sold. The authorization token stays on the author's computer. The
tool uses the [YouTube API Services](https://developers.google.com/youtube): by using it,
the author agrees to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and
data is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The
authorization can be revoked at any time in [the Google account security
settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Storage and deletion.** The statistics read by the tool are stored only on the author's
computer, for his own use. If the authorization is revoked, the authorization token and
the stored statistics are deleted. Since the tool stores no data about anyone else, there
is no other data to delete; any question can be sent through the [About]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/about.html) page.
