reuse them, crediting the author and sharing alike. The sources of every figure are given
with each video.

## The "Audience" tool

"Audience" is the author's personal tool, running on his computer. It is not offered,
sold or distributed to anyone: its only user is the author, on his own YouTube channels.
It uses the [YouTube API Services](https://developers.google.com/youtube) to read these
channels' statistics and to publish the author's videos on them.

By using this tool, its user agrees to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms);
data is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
What the tool reads and keeps is described on the [Privacy]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/privacy.html)
page. Access can be revoked at any time in [the Google account security
settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
